This project is a custom ECG (Electrocardiogram) front-end circuit designed to read very small electrical signals produced by the human heart.


The signal is:



captured from electrodes on the skin,



amplified using an instrumentation amplifier,



cleaned with a series of biomedical filters (HPF, LPF, BRF),



and prepared for digitization by a microcontroller or ADC.



The PCB is designed in KiCad and implemented on a 4-layer stack-up for improved signal integrity, grounding, and noise performance.
